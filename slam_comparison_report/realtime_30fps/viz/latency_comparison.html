What the time is spent on

ORB-SLAM3 stages come from its own REGISTER_TIMES instrumentation; RTAB-Map's from OdomInfo.

These two numbers are not the same measurement

ORB-SLAM3's figure is TrackRGBD() — which already includes local-map tracking and pose optimisation. RTAB-Map's is Odometry::process() only; its mapping, loop closure and graph optimisation run on a separate thread at Rtabmap/DetectionRate = 1 Hz and never appear here. That separation is RTAB-Map's design, not an oversight — but it means RTAB-Map is doing less work per frame and still taking longer.

For the same reason ORB-SLAM3's mapping cost has to be quoted separately: its Local Mapping thread spent 128 ms mean per keyframe (p95 262 ms), dominated by local bundle adjustment. It did not stall tracking at this map size — 139 keyframes, 12k map points — but it is the thing that would at a larger one.
